一段收集到的声明

发表于 2026-11-14 阅读次数： 本文字数： 2k 阅读时长 ≈ 7 分钟

GPT目前的最新模型都喜欢免责声明，大家都说它不好好说话。于是收集到了下面一段话：

需要首先说明的是,以下表述仅旨在就相关问题提供一种可能的、有限的、非穷尽性的分析视角,而不应被理解为对任何特定事实、结论、立场、意图或结果的无条件确认;当然,这并不意味着我否认相关事实、拒绝形成判断,或主张任何相反结论,只是说,在缺乏进一步背景信息、完整上下文及可独立验证的证据之前,我无法合理地排除其他解释的可能性,也不宜将当前表述视为唯一、最终或具有普遍适用性的答案。严格来说,即使在现有信息足以支持某种初步判断的情况下,这种支持也仅限于当前可获得的信息范围之内,并不必然延伸至尚未明确的情境、未被讨论的例外情况,或未来可能出现的新证据;但与此同时,我也不希望因为强调这些限制,而使人误以为现有信息毫无价值,或者任何判断都同样合理。换言之,我既不主张这一结论必然成立,也不主张它必然不成立;既不排除其在特定条件下成立的可能性,也不保证这些条件已经得到满足;既不能仅凭现有材料确认其完全正确,也不能仅凭尚存的不确定性断言其错误。需要特别指出的是,上述限定本身同样受到信息范围、语境理解及表述方式的限制,因此不应被视为对所有潜在例外的完整列举;如果其中某些限定看起来过于谨慎,这并不必然意味着相关问题本身极其复杂,也可能只是因为目前没有充分理由将其简化为更强、更明确的断言。反过来说,如果某些表述看起来相对明确,也不应据此推断我已排除所有替代解释,或愿意对该表述在任何语境下的适用性承担无条件责任。对于可能产生的误解,我愿意在获得更明确的语境后作进一步澄清,但这一意愿本身并不构成对任何特定解释、后续结果或澄清效果的保证;同样,澄清也不必然意味着原表述存在错误,正如原表述未被证明错误,也不意味着它已经得到了充分证明。最后,为避免对以上内容作出超出其实际含义的推断,我想强调:这段话既不是对某一具体结论的完全肯定,也不是对该结论的完全否定,而是在现有条件下,对能够表达什么、不能确定什么,以及为什么暂时不宜作出更强断言所进行的一种有限说明。当然,这一说明本身也并非不受限制;如果需要,还可以进一步限定其适用范围,但是否有必要如此,仍取决于具体语境、实际目的,以及对表述精确程度的要求。以上仅供参考,不构成任何形式的绝对承诺;如有不同理解,应以相关事实、具体语境及进一步核实的结果为准,而不宜仅依据本段文字本身作出超出其明确含义的推断。

这段话感觉是一段英语学习的绝佳例子，所以我让GPT翻译回英语：

Of course, this does not mean that I deny the relevant facts, refuse to form a judgment, or assert any contrary conclusion. Rather, it simply means that, in the absence of further background information, complete contextual understanding, and independently verifiable evidence, I cannot reasonably rule out the possibility of alternative interpretations, nor would it be appropriate to regard the present statement as the sole, definitive, or universally applicable answer.

Strictly speaking, even if the information currently available is sufficient to support a preliminary judgment, such support is necessarily limited to the scope of that information and does not automatically extend to circumstances that have yet to be clarified, exceptions that have not been discussed, or new evidence that may emerge in the future. At the same time, I do not wish to create the impression, merely by emphasizing these limitations, that the existing information is entirely without value or that all possible judgments are equally reasonable.

In other words, I neither claim that this conclusion is necessarily true nor that it is necessarily false. I neither exclude the possibility that it may hold under certain conditions nor guarantee that those conditions have actually been satisfied. I cannot confirm, solely on the basis of the available materials, that it is entirely correct, but neither can I conclude that it is incorrect merely because some degree of uncertainty remains.

It is particularly important to note that the foregoing qualifications are themselves subject to limitations arising from the scope of available information, the interpretation of context, and the manner in which they are expressed. Accordingly, they should not be regarded as an exhaustive enumeration of all potential exceptions. If some of these qualifications appear excessively cautious, this does not necessarily imply that the underlying issue is exceptionally complex. Conversely, if certain statements appear relatively clear or definitive, this should not be taken to mean that I have ruled out all alternative interpretations or that I am willing to assume unconditional responsibility for the applicability of those statements in every possible context.

With respect to any potential misunderstandings, I am willing to provide further clarification once a more precise context becomes available. However, this willingness does not, in itself, constitute a guarantee of any particular interpretation, subsequent outcome, or effectiveness of such clarification. Likewise, providing clarification does not necessarily imply that the original statement was incorrect, just as the fact that the original statement has not been proven incorrect does not mean that it has been sufficiently substantiated.

Rather, it is a limited explanation, under the present circumstances, of what can reasonably be stated, what cannot yet be established with certainty, and why stronger assertions may not be warranted at this stage.

In the event of differing interpretations, reference should be made to the relevant facts, the specific context, and the results of further verification, rather than relying solely on this passage to draw inferences that exceed its explicitly stated meaning.

当然，最搞笑的是，GPT在翻译完以后，给我来了一段下面的话：

从文本特征判断，原文可能是一段刻意模仿人工智能回答中过度谨慎、反复保留意见的英文段落。其显著特点是，每提出一个判断，就立即限定该判断的适用范围，再对限定本身增加新的限定。不过，单凭中文译文，无法确定其是否确实出自英文，或是否由人工智能生成。
