一些碎片。

昨天翻出了个古老的USB2.0的硬盘。发现好多好古老的东西。其中不少是求学时代留下来的东西。现在搜了下，这些求学时代的有些东西也很难搜到了。

虽然现在是大模型时代，很多知识，特别是公开的，基本都能搜到。但是似乎少了点什么，那就是有些现在找的资料，没有我当初学习时的情景以及经历。

本篇随便存一些当初学习时候有点意思的东西。

GRE 旧题

明媚的忧伤

Parts of seventeenth-century Chinese pleasure gardens were not necessarily intended to look (i) _____; they were designed expressly to evoke the agreeable melancholy resulting from a sense of the (ii) _____ of natural beauty and human glory.

Blank(i)
A. cheerful  B. luxuriant  C. collusive
Blank(ii)
D. transitoriness  E. taciturnity  F. frivolity

Answer: AD

The cultivation of the emotion of natsukashii, interpretable as “pleasant sorrow,” brings Japanese to Kyoto in the spring, not to ___ the cherry blossoms in full bloom but to ___ the fading, falling flowers.

Blank(i)
A. mourn  B. arrange  C. savor
Blank(ii)
D. rejoice over  E. grieve over  F. preserve

Answer: DE
